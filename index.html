Our story

A shop that started with my mother's cupboard

I grew up watching my mother fold her sarees one by one and tell me which thread was cotton, which was silk, and how to know the difference with just a touch. In 2017, I opened Hingula on Dabadi Road with that same habit.

The name comes from hingul, the deep red we put in the parting of the hair and on festival thalis. It reminds me that clothes are part of our celebrations, not just something we buy.

Every kurti and saree here is something I would be happy to see my sister wear. If it does not feel right in your hand, I will tell you honestly.

- Owner, Hingula
